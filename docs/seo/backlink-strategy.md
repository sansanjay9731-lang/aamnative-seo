## TIER 2 BACKLINKS — 10 Directories to Submit Today

### 1. Google Business Profile (business.google.com)
- Create listing: Business name (AamNative), category (Online Grocery Store / Fruit Retailer), phone, website, hours
- Upload 10+ product photos of mangoes, packaging, delivery boxes
- Add services: "Alphonso Mango Delivery," "GI Certified Mangoes," "Bulk Mango Orders"
- Get 10 initial reviews from existing customers — critical for Local Pack ranking
- Post weekly updates during mango season (March–June)

### 2. APEDA (apeda.gov.in) — DA ~68
- Register as Indian agricultural producer/exporter
- Submit: RCMC certificate application, product details, GI cert number, company PAN, bank details
- Also list on APEDA's AgriXchange portal for export buyer visibility
- High trust signal — government portal backlink

### 3. FSSAI FoSCoS Portal (foscos.fssai.gov.in)
- Ensure FSSAI license number is active and publicly searchable
- Public-facing licensee directory gives a citation backlink
- Apply for "Eat Right India" partner program listing

### 4. IndiaMART (indiamart.com) — DA ~72
- Create supplier profile under: Fresh Fruits > Mangoes > Alphonso Mangoes
- Submit: product catalog with photos, MOQ, price range, certifications (GI, FSSAI), delivery areas
- Add keywords: "Ratnagiri Alphonso," "GI certified," "farm direct"
- One of the strongest Indian directory links available

### 5. TradeIndia (tradeindia.com) — DA ~58
- Create supplier listing under Fresh Agricultural Products
- Submit: company profile, product images, GI certificate details, FSSAI number
- Fill all optional fields — complete profiles rank higher internally

### 6. Justdial (justdial.com) — DA ~68
- List under: Fruits & Vegetables Online, Online Grocery, Mango Suppliers
- Submit: business name, phone, website, address, business hours, payment methods

### 7. Sulekha Business (sulekha.com) — DA ~55
- Create listing under: Food & Grocery > Fruit Suppliers

### 8. Yellow Pages India (yellowpages.in) — DA ~45

### 9. Startup India Portal (startupindia.gov.in) — DA ~68
- If registered as startup, list on DPIIT Startup India database
- High authority government backlink
- Apply for "Food & Agriculture" sector recognition

### 10. Amazon Brand Registry + Flipkart Seller Hub — DA ~90+

## QUORA STRATEGY — 5 Questions to Answer With Links

**Question 1:** "Where can I buy authentic Ratnagiri Alphonso mangoes online in India?"
- Write 400-word answer: what makes Ratnagiri Alphonso unique, how to identify fakes, what to look for in online seller (FSSAI license, GI cert). Drop aamnative.com naturally, not as first sentence.

**Question 2:** "Is there a difference between Devgad and Ratnagiri Alphonso mangoes?"
- Write detailed educational comparison (origin, taste, texture, price). Natural link: "If you want to try Ratnagiri-certified stock, [aamnative.com] lists their GI certificate number on the product page."

**Question 3:** "How do I know if Alphonso mangoes I'm buying online are genuine?"
- List 5 ways to verify authenticity (GI cert, FSSAI number, farm photos, natural ripening, seasonal availability March-June only).

**Question 4:** "What is the price of Ratnagiri Alphonso mangoes per dozen in 2025/2026?"
- Give realistic price breakdown (₹600–₹1200/dozen depending on grade), explain premium justification.

**Question 5:** "Are Alphonso mangoes good for health? What are their nutritional benefits?"
- Nutrition-focused answer (Vitamin A, C, folate, antioxidants, digestive enzymes). Cite ICMR/NIN data. End: "For maximum nutritional value, choose tree-ripened Alphonso from GI-certified origins."
